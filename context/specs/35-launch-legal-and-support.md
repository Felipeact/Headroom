# Spec 35: Legal pages and support

- **Milestone:** 7, production
- **Status:** Not started
- **Depends on:** 07 (can start early)

## Goal

Publish the policies and the support channel that clients need before they share bank data.

## Build

- Pages for the privacy policy, terms of service, and data-handling policy, linked from the app's footer, from sign-up, and from emails.
  - A lawyer reviews the text under PIPEDA and the BC and Alberta privacy laws.
  - The privacy policy says that data is stored in the United States.
- A support email address.
- A way to request a data export or deletion. In v1, the team handles these requests by hand.

## Done when

- [ ] The pages are live on staging, and linked from the footer and from sign-up.
- [ ] A deletion request reaches the support inbox.
